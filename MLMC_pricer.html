Autocallable Structured Note Pricer with MLMC

Posted by Gary Pai

  • The Autocall Level (The "Exit"): At pre-defined observation dates (quarterly in our code), the spot price of the underlying asset (S) is compared to the Strike (K). If S ≥ K, the contract "calls" automatically. The investor receives their initial principal plus a pro-rated coupon and the contract terminates.
  • The Contingent Coupon (q): This is the "reward." Unlike a standard bond that pays regardless of market conditions, this coupon is often only paid if the note autocalls or if the asset remains above a certain level.
  • The Protection Barrier (p): This is the "safety net." If the note is never called and reaches maturity (T), the investor is still protected as long as the asset price hasn't fallen below the barrier (e.g., 70% of the initial price).
Input:
Initial underlying asset price
Autocall Level
Time to maturity Years
Risk free interest rate (annulized)
Coupon Rate (q)
Volatility (annulized)
Max Level (typically 3 to 5)
Protection Barrier (p)
Base Samples (int >500)
Output:
Autocallable value

The calculation is based on MLMC.
