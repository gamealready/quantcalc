What can QuantCalc do for you?

QuantCalc is a website that specializes in calibration, Paper and derivative pricing solutions. It is web-based service providing valuations, calibrations, Paper detections. In QuantCalc users can input their parameters to calculate the derivative price. In some calculators, QuantCalc can even collect the market data automatically, and price or calibrate with market data. The calculators are categorized by purposes on the top of the website and by methods and markets on the right hand side.
